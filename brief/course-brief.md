# Course Brief

## Topic

AI engineer

## Target Audience

Experienced software developers diverting to AI engineering. These are highly technical people, but from different background. 

## Prerequisites

- Basic machine learning and llm understanding

- Experience with docker and local deployment

- working with coding agents

- Planning system architectures, micro services

- assume they have access to local docker, hugging face paid account, google collab paid account

## Desired Outcome

Students can apply for professional AI engineers jobs. you need to make sure they stand in the job requirements, so do a little research job before you start. 

## Duration

8-12 modules, ~8-12 hours total

## Special instructions

You are going solo, building the course end to end. you can make minor changes to the duration, content etc.
