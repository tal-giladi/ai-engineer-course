# Module 12 quiz — Capstone, Portfolio & Interview Readiness

Ten scenario questions on the judgment around finishing and presenting your work: triaging a capstone against a definition of done, choosing what to document and what to cut, surviving the depth ladder in a project deep-dive, and working an AI system design round where requirements, permissions, evaluation and cost decide the design. They test reasoning, not recall. Most show a repository, an interview exchange or a design-round moment and ask what the strongest move is.

**Covers**

- [12.1 · Shipping the Capstone](../lessons/module-12/lesson-01.md) — the two failure patterns, the definition of done, the clean-clone test, depth and range.
- [12.2 · Documenting Like a Hire: Model Card, Eval Report, Architecture Doc](../lessons/module-12/lesson-02.md) — the four-minute README, the eval report and its reverts, model cards, decision records.
- [12.3 · The Project Deep-Dive Round](../lessons/module-12/lesson-03.md) — the five-part structure, the depth ladder, the five stories, saying "I don't know".
- [12.4 · The AI System Design Round](../lessons/module-12/lesson-04.md) — the seven steps, the cost model out loud, the evaluation plan, the traps and curveballs.

**Pass mark:** 70% (7 of 10). Unlimited retries; answers and explanations appear after you submit.

The real assessment of this module is your capstone repository, graded against the [capstone rubric](../assessments/capstone-rubric.md).
