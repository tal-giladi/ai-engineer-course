# Module 5 quiz — Retrieval II: Production RAG

Ten scenario questions on taking a RAG pipeline from "it answers" to something you can diagnose, trust and defend: localizing failures by stage, fixing the query, reranking and assembling context, grounding with validated citations and refusal, and reading the four RAG metrics as a diagnosis. They test reasoning, not recall. Most give you a metric vector, a measurement table or a failing answer and ask what it shows or what to do next, the way the AI system design round does.

**Covers**

- [05.1 · The RAG Failure Taxonomy](../lessons/module-05/lesson-01.md) — four stages, four failure modes, the diagnostic tool and its four decisive fields.
- [05.2 · Query Rewriting and Multi-Query Retrieval](../lessons/module-05/lesson-02.md) — conversational rewriting, decomposition, multi-query, HyDE, and rejecting techniques on cost.
- [05.3 · Reranking and Context Assembly](../lessons/module-05/lesson-03.md) — the bi-encoder to cross-encoder funnel, deduplication, ordering for attention, why more context is often worse.
- [05.4 · Grounding, Citations, and Knowing When to Refuse](../lessons/module-05/lesson-04.md) — enforced grounding, mechanical citation validation, missed versus false refusals.
- [05.5 · Measuring RAG: Faithfulness, Context Precision, Context Recall](../lessons/module-05/lesson-05.md) — the four metrics as judge outputs, the diagnostic matrix, per-stratum reading.

**Pass mark:** 70% (7 of 10). Unlimited retries; answers and explanations appear after you submit.

The hands-on triage exercise is in [the Module 5 practical](../projects/module-05-practical.md).
