# Module 3 quiz — Evaluation Foundations

Ten scenario questions on the evaluation harness every later module is measured against: why you evaluate before you build, golden datasets, metrics that fit the task, calibrated LLM judges and CI gates. They test reasoning, not recall. Most give you a result, a judge or a gate and ask what it shows, what is wrong with it, or what to do next. This is the module interviewers probe most, so it is worth getting right.

**Covers**

- [03.1 · Why You Evaluate Before You Build](../lessons/module-03/lesson-01.md) — the vibes trap, three evaluation surfaces, the charter and its priority ordering.
- [03.2 · Building a Golden Dataset](../lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md) — small stratified sets, sizing and resolution, rubric agreement, held-out discipline.
- [03.3 · Metrics That Fit the Task](../lessons/module-03/lesson-03.md) — why accuracy misleads, metrics by task shape, cost per successful task.
- [03.4 · LLM-as-Judge and Judge Calibration](../lessons/module-03/lesson-04.md) — pairwise judging, the biases and their fixes, calibration and the trust boundary.
- [03.5 · Regression Suites and CI Gating](../lessons/module-03/lesson-05.md) — smoke and full tiers, gating on a band, hard floors, Promptfoo thresholds.

**Pass mark:** 70% (7 of 10). Unlimited retries; answers and explanations appear after you submit.

The hands-on summarizer evaluation is in [the Module 3 practical](../projects/module-03-practical.md).
